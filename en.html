vc-rs

RVC voice changer for Windows

Convert microphone input or WAV files into another voice using an RVC model. Written in Rust, with a desktop GUI, a CLI and a VST3 plugin.

Windows x64 / Japanese & English UI / MIT License

Downloads Source on GitHub

Bring your own RVC voice model. Prebuilt downloads are available; no build required.

Features

Real-time conversion
The main screen brings together voice model, audio input/output, volume and pitch controls. Volume and pitch update live, and your settings are reused next time.
Model setup
Supports ONNX models and in-app conversion of supported RVC v2 / F0 .pth models. Download auxiliary models through Setup or “Model settings”, or select custom files.
WAV files and DAWs
Convert WAV files with the included CLI, or use the VST3 plugin inside your DAW.

Getting started

  1. Extract the ZIP and open vc-gui.exe

    Keep the DLLs in the same folder. On first launch, choose your language and review the terms of use.

  2. Set up your microphone and voice model

    Follow the setup guide to check audio input and output, then select an RVC model. Download any missing auxiliary models from the app.

  3. Finish preparation and open the main screen

    Once the required models and runtime components are ready, select “Open main screen” to save your settings. Conversion does not start at this point.

  4. Select “Start” on the main screen

    Listen to the converted audio and adjust volume and pitch. Changes that require a restart, such as model or device changes, are indicated at the top of the screen. Select “Restart” to apply them, or “Stop” to end conversion.

Connection and denoising options are under “Audio devices and noise reduction”; GPU and latency settings are under “Backend Details”. Scroll to the bottom of the main screen to change the language or reopen Setup.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

vc-rs is free, open-source software under the MIT License. Voice models and auxiliary models have their own terms of use.

Are voice models included?

No. Bring your own RVC voice model and check its terms of use. ContentVec and RMVPE auxiliary models can be downloaded from the app.

Can I use any .pth model?

The built-in converter supports RVC v2 / F0 models. Other models, such as v1, need to be converted to ONNX beforehand. See model requirements.

Can I use it with Discord or OBS?

Route converted audio through a virtual audio device. Set the vc-rs output to its playback endpoint, then select the paired recording endpoint as the microphone in Discord or the audio input in OBS. Driver installation and configuration of the receiving app are manual.

What if the audio stutters or is delayed?

Adjust chunk size under “Backend Details” on the main screen. Start with stable settings, then reduce the chunk size to lower latency and select “Restart” to apply the change. Results depend on your GPU, model and configuration. Read the tuning guide.
